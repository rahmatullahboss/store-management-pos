Questions retailers ask before changing systems.

Clear boundaries make a safer implementation.

Can the POS continue when the internet connection drops?

The product is designed around a controlled offline operating mode. Eligible operations are stored in a durable local queue, the interface clearly shows offline risk, and synchronisation is idempotent when connectivity returns. The exact offline scope should be confirmed during onboarding.

Can one account manage multiple stores and warehouses?

Yes. Store, warehouse, register, legal entity, timezone and business date are first-class dimensions. Plan limits define the included operating scale, while larger rollouts are quoted separately.

Does it include accounting, not only sales reports?

The platform scope includes payment allocation, refunds, settlements, double-entry accounting, receivables, payables, banking, reconciliation and period controls. Each rollout should enable modules in a controlled sequence based on readiness.

Will existing product and stock data be migrated?

Migration can cover products, variants, barcodes, units, opening stock, suppliers, customers and selected opening balances. Data quality, mapping and verification effort are assessed and quoted separately.

Is it suitable for restaurants or pharmacies?

The initial core targets general retail, grocery without complex regulated workflows, fashion, electronics, home goods, cosmetics, specialty retail and basic wholesale. Restaurant, pharmacy, fuel, hospitality, manufacturing and repair workflows require later extension packs.
